## 1. Getting Started

### Installing the App

**Android:**
1. Download **Ubumwe Connect** from the Google Play Store
2. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to complete
3. Tap **Open** to launch the app

**iOS:**
1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Search for "Ubumwe Connect"
3. Tap the cloud download icon to install

**Web:**
Visit [app.ubumweconnect.rw](https://app.ubumweconnect.rw) in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

### Minimum Requirements
- Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher
- iOS 13.0 or higher
- Stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)

## 2. Account Setup

### Creating an Account

1. Open Ubumwe Connect
2. Watch the **onboarding screens** to learn about the app's features
3. Tap **Get Started** on the last onboarding page
4. Tap **Create Account**
5. Fill in:
   - **Email Address** — your personal email
   - **Username** — a unique handle (letters, numbers, underscores only)
   - **Display Name** — how others will see you
   - **Password** — minimum 8 characters, include uppercase, number, and special character
   - **Preferred Language** — the language you want to receive messages in
6. Optionally add a **Profile Photo** by tapping the camera icon
7. Tap **Create Account**
8. Verify your email if prompted

### Signing In with Google

1. On the login screen, tap **Continue with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. On your first login, choose your **Preferred Language**
4. Tap **Continue**

### Logging In

1. Open the app
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Tap **Sign In**

If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot Password?**, enter your email, and check your inbox for a reset link.

## 3. Home Screen & Navigation

The home screen has 3 tabs at the bottom:

| Tab | Icon | Description |
|-----|------|-------------|
| **Chats** | 💬 | All your conversations |
| **Contacts** | 👥 | Your contacts list |
| **Profile** | 👤 | Your profile and settings |

### Chats Tab

The Chats tab shows all your conversations sorted by most recent activity. Each conversation shows:
- **Avatar** — contact's photo or group icon
- **Name** — contact's display name or group name
- **Last message** — already translated into your language
- **Time** — when the last message was sent
- **Unread badge** — orange number showing unread messages

Use the **sub-tabs** to filter: **All** · **Direct** · **Groups**

## 4. Messaging

### Starting a New Conversation

**Direct Message:**
1. Tap the **compose icon** (pencil) on the Chats tab
2. Select **Direct Message**
3. Choose a contact from the list
4. Tap **Create**

**Group Chat:**
1. Tap the compose icon
2. Select **Group**
3. Enter a **Group Name**
4. Select multiple contacts by tapping them
5. Tap **Create**

### Sending Messages

1. Open a conversation
2. Tap the **text field** at the bottom
3. Type your message in **any language you prefer**
4. Tap the **send button** (blue arrow)

Your message will be automatically translated for all participants in the conversation.

### Sending Voice Messages

1. Tap and **hold the microphone icon** in the message bar
2. Speak your message
3. **Release** to send, or **slide left** to cancel
4. The voice message will be transcribed and translated automatically

### Sending Images & Files

1. Tap the **paperclip/attachment icon**
2. Choose from:
   - **Camera** — take a photo now
   - **Gallery** — pick from your photos
   - **File** — choose any file

### Replying to a Message

1. **Long-press** on any message
2. Tap **Reply**
3. Type your reply — the original message will appear as a quote

### Deleting a Message

1. **Long-press** on your own message
2. Tap **Delete**
3. Confirm — deleted messages show as "This message was deleted"

## 5. Voice & Video Calls

### Making a Call

**From a conversation:**
1. Open the conversation
2. Tap the **phone icon** (voice) or **camera icon** (video) in the top-right

**From a contact's profile:**
1. Open the contact's profile
2. Tap **Voice Call** or **Video** button

### During a Voice Call

The call screen shows:
- Your contact's name and avatar
- **Live subtitles** — real-time translation of what's being said
- Call duration

**Controls:**
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 🎤 Mute | Mute/unmute your microphone |
| 🔊 Speaker | Switch to/from speaker |
| 📝 Subtitles | Toggle live subtitle translation |
| 📹 Camera | Switch to video (voice calls only) |
| 🔴 End | End the call |

### During a Video Call

- You appear in a **small box** (bottom-right)
- The other person fills the screen
- Tap anywhere to show/hide controls (they auto-hide after 3 seconds)
- **Live captions** appear at the bottom of the screen

### Receiving a Call

When someone calls you:
1. Your phone rings and shows the **incoming call screen**
2. Tap the **green button** to accept
3. Tap the **red button** to decline

### Finding Your Contacts Tab

Tap **Contacts** (👥) at the bottom of the screen.

Your contacts are listed alphabetically with their:
- Profile photo
- Display name and username

2. Type a name or username
3. Results filter in real time

1. Tap the **person+ icon** in the top-right of the Contacts tab

3. Find the person in the results

Tap the **chat icon** next to any contact, or tap their name to view their profile, then tap **Message**.

## 7. Translation Features

### How Automatic Translation Works

Ubumwe Connect automatically translates every message into your preferred language. You don't need to do anything — it happens instantly in the background.

For example:
- Amara sends "Muraho!" in Kinyarwanda
- James (English) sees "Hello!" in the chat
- Marie (French) sees "Bonjour!" in the same conversation

### Seeing the Original Message

If you want to see what someone originally wrote:
1. **Tap** on any translated message
2. The original text appears below the translation

A small **🌐 Translated from Kinyarwanda** label shows under translated messages.

### Changing Your Preferred Language

1. Go to **Profile** tab → tap **Language Settings**
2. Tap your new preferred language
3. Tap **Save** — all future messages will be displayed in your new language

You can also access this from **Settings → My Language**.

### Supported Languages

| Language | Flag | Code |
|----------|------|------|
| Kinyarwanda | 🇷🇼 | kinyarwanda |
| English | 🇬🇧 | english |
| French | 🇫🇷 | french |
| Swahili | 🇹🇿 | swahili |

### Translation Options

In **Settings → Translation Options**:

- **Auto-Translate Messages** — turn translation on or off
- **Show Original Text** — always show original alongside translation

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Messages are stored securely on our servers. We never sell your data.

**Q: Can I use the app without internet?**  
A: No — Ubumwe Connect requires an internet connection for real-time messaging and translation.

**Q: How accurate are the translations?**  
A: Translations use OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini, which is highly accurate for the supported languages. Technical or slang terms may occasionally be imperfect.

**Q: Does Ubumwe Connect support more than 4 languages?**  
A: Currently we support Kinyarwanda, English, French, and Swahili. More languages are planned for future releases.

**Q: Are voice/video calls free?**  
A: Calls use your internet data. Standard mobile data rates apply. The app itself is free.

**Q: Can I use one account on multiple devices?**  
A: Yes — sign in with your credentials on any device. Your messages sync automatically.

## 10. Troubleshooting

### Messages Not Translating

1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your preferred language is set: **Settings → My Language**
3. Ensure **Auto-Translate Messages** is enabled in Settings
4. Force close and reopen the app

### Cannot Connect to Voice/Video Call

1. Ensure the app has **Microphone** and **Camera** permissions:
   - Android: Settings → Apps → Ubumwe Connect → Permissions
   - iOS: Settings → Privacy → Microphone / Camera
2. Check your internet connection (calls require stable connection)
3. Try switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data or vice versa

### App Crashes or Freezes

1. Force close the app
2. Restart your device
3. Check for app updates in the app store
4. If the problem persists, reinstall the app (your data is saved in the cloud)

### Forgot Password

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email
4. Follow the link in the email to set a new password

### Contact Support

If you can't resolve an issue:
- **Email:** support@ubumweconnect.rw
- **In-App:** Profile → Help & Support
- **GitHub Issues:** github.com/yourorg/ubumweconnect/issues
